## Scope and method

The audit used the public high-quality model-world ride below in headed Chrome
153 on macOS. The browser reported 8 logical CPUs, 16 GB device memory, WebGL 2,
DPR 1, a 1888 × 1339 viewport and a 1888 × 1285 drawing buffer. The host was not
isolated, so these numbers are diagnostic measurements rather than a
cross-machine release threshold.

`https://zagreb.lol/prijevoz/utp/?project=13&lang=hr&st3d=planner-cab&line=1&offset=559.7&dir=1`

The run included:

- two supplied screenshots at chainages 0+530 and 0+560;
- a cold opening sampled every 100 ms from the loading hold through reveal;
- a controlled 15 s, 208.37 m replay from chainage 500 at 50 km/h;
- a 10 s stationary sample after the streamed queues drained;
- exact pose, authored elevation, intended track offset and queue snapshots from
  the public Station3D diagnostics API.

The frame-rate result is good on this machine once work is admitted: movement
only raised p99 by 7.4 ms and did not reduce the average below 60 fps. The
primary failure is not steady rendering throughput. It is generation
coherency and latency: the curtain reveals a predecessor world while newer
rail/ground work is pending, and a 50 km/h vehicle can outrun the successor
publication backlog.

The implemented correctness gate was then tested through a locally vendored,
built Station3D package in the Prijevoz consumer. In a repeat cold headed run
the curtain first painted at 0.19 s and was removed at 39.11 s, after the
current ground generation settled. At chainage 0+560 the resulting corridor
had continuous ground and formation geometry and the visible rails were
centred under the cab. The old 19.41 s reveal was therefore about 19.7 s too
early on this host. That delay is a measured correctness cost, not a rendering
throughput regression, and is why the local-support token is the next P1 item.

The cold run issued 250 recorded resource requests. Resource Timing exposed
4.45 MB encoded and 20.22 MB decoded response bodies; cached and cross-origin
entries made transfer-size totals incomplete. `performance.memory` reported a
very large heap after repeated long route/reload work, but that browser-level
number is not accepted as a Station3D memory measurement. A fresh-process,
repeatable lifecycle capture is required before setting a memory budget.

## Root causes of the reported defects

### Mixed ground and rail generations at reveal

The supplied screenshots show two different symptoms of one publication
problem. At 0+530 the vehicle/camera is on the intended right-hand alignment
while the visible rails remain to its left. At 0+560 terrain and formation
triangles from incompatible stages cross the view. After the queues settle at
the exact same chainage, the rails are centred and the ground is complete.

The route-coordinate test measured **0.00 m** discrepancy between the live
vehicle pose and the planner's intended right-hand track centre. The offset is
therefore not generated by the vehicle pose. The visible world is behind it.

The opening contract explains why. `initialGroundSupportReady()` treated any
one completed ground publication as ready, even when the coordinator explicitly
reported successor changes. In the measured cold run the curtain dropped with
three ground changes pending. The engine fix now requires the current
generation to be settled before the initial world-data hold can complete.

### Cab height and pitch used different parameterisations

The planner supplied pitch from its distance-parameterized elevation table but
recomputed height by projecting the moving horizontal point back onto the
source track. The two paths diverged by **0.148 m** in the audited 500–708 m
window. Height also rounded to centimetres before the engine's camera smoother.
That combination can move the camera vertically while its grade instrument
continues to show an almost level authored chord.

The downstream adapter fix now uses the same interpolated profile sample for
height and pitch, preserves its precision, and removes one nearest-track search
per articulated car per frame.

The fixed build was sampled every 5 m from chainage 520 through 700. Pose height
and profile height agreed exactly except for four sub-rounding differences of
0.00000143 m; the maximum measured disagreement was therefore **0.00143 mm**,
down from 148 mm. Pitch changed smoothly into a stable −0.05896° grade instead
of being combined with a separately projected height.

## Ranked improvements

Effort is focused engineering plus deterministic tests and the relevant headed
check: S is hours, M is roughly one to two focused days, L is several days, and
XL is a project. Payoff is relative to the measured failure, not a generic FPS
claim.

| Priority | Improvement | Effort | Risk | Expected payoff |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P0 — implemented | Require the current ground generation before the initial reveal. | S | Medium: the measured cold hold grew from 19.41 s to 39.11 s; a genuinely stuck coordinator can hold until the existing absolute failsafe. | Prevents the mixed predecessor/successor world that produced missing ground and apparently offset rails. |
| P0 — implemented downstream | Drive planner-cab height and pitch from one chainage profile; keep full precision. | S | Low: absolute/relative datum contracts are unchanged. | Removes the measured 0.148 m height/pitch disagreement, centimetre stepping and repeated nearest-track queries. |
| P1 | Replace the conservative whole-generation opening gate with a revisioned **local support readiness** token covering the observer and first braking-distance corridor. | M | Medium: an incomplete dependency set can reintroduce holes; needs terrain, rails, roads, openings and physics tests. | Keeps the P0 correctness guarantee while recovering most added startup latency. |
| P1 | Coalesce invalidations while the curtain is frozen and suppress obsolete intermediate generations before compilation/publication. | L | High: source lifetime, rollback and retained-reader rules are tightly coupled. | Attacks the 3–7 ground changes and 81–196 surface publications seen around reveal/movement instead of merely waiting for them. |
| P1 | Prewarm a bounded route-ahead corridor from starting speed and braking distance, with rail/ground priority over scenery. | M | Medium: corridor bounds and eviction must remain finite for arbitrary hosts. | Reduces the chance that a 50 km/h cab reaches cells before their coherent formation is published. |
| P2 | Defer non-critical detailed buildings, façades and decor until the observer's physical ground/rail revision is accepted. | M | Medium: can create controlled scenery pop and must preserve building support dependencies. | Converts work now competing during the 19.4 s opening and the 53-building replay peak into lower-priority streaming. |
| P2 | Make vertical and angular smoothing time-based, and expose raw/target/smoothed camera values in diagnostics. | S–M | Low. | Identical ride feel at 30, 60 and 120 Hz, plus direct regression evidence instead of inferring camera motion from screenshots. |
| P2 | Restore a package-owned headed benchmark with fresh-process memory, renderer counters, cold/warm cases and machine metadata. | M | Low. | Makes before/after claims reproducible now that the old downstream-only profiler did not survive extraction. |
| P2 | Add bounded lifecycle memory acceptance: open, drive, settle, close, reopen, and return to the same cell. | M | Low to medium. | Detects retained CPU/GPU resources and validates eviction without relying on the unqualified heap observation from this audit. |
| P3 | Precompute stable geometry only after the queue/coherency work above. | XL | High: data/version/API and rollback surface. | Potentially removes repeated browser compilation, but older baking evidence found no consistent speedup, so it is not the next fix. |

## Acceptance for the P0 fixes

Before release, repeat the exact public-path scenario from a packed Station3D
consumer build and require all of the following:

1. the curtain never drops while the current initial ground generation is
   pending;
2. the view at 0+530, 0+560 and 0+620 contains continuous terrain and centred
   visible rails;
3. pose height equals the line-profile height to floating-point tolerance, and
   its finite-difference slope agrees with the authored pitch chord;
4. the vehicle's horizontal pose remains on the intended right-hand track
   centre;
5. moving p95/p99 and long-interval counts do not regress materially against
   the figures above;
6. after pause, ground, surface, building and compiler queues drain to zero;
7. a clean consumer can pack, install, vendor and open the same ride.

The stricter opening gate deliberately trades 19.7 s in this cold run for a
valid world. The P1 local-revision gate is the correct way to recover that
time; the engine must not restore the old “any publication is enough” shortcut.
