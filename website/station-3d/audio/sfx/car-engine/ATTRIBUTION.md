# Car engine sample bank

`loop-0.wav` through `loop-5.wav` are the six files from “racing car engine
sound loops” by domasx2 on OpenGameArt. They were made from a public-domain car
recording, edited as short loops at increasing pitches, and released under
CC0 1.0. Attribution is not required, but the source is retained here so the
license trail stays explicit.

- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/racing-car-engine-sound-loops
- License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Original filenames: `loop_0.wav`, `loop_1.wav`, `loop_2.wav`, `loop_3.wav`,
  `loop_4.wav`, `loop_5.wav`
