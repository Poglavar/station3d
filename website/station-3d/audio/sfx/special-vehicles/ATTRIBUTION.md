# Special-vehicle audio

These come from public HQ MP3 previews of CC0 1.0 recordings hosted by
Freesound. Attribution is not required, but the provenance is retained here.
The propeller and wake loops are unmodified previews.

- `airplane-propeller-loop.mp3`: “Airplane Propeller Loop” by modusmogulus,
  https://freesound.org/people/modusmogulus/sounds/789390/
- `boat-engine-loop.mp3`: “Yanmar_Engine_Slow.mp3” by ivolipa, a Yanmar boat
  diesel recorded on a voyage near the island of Vis,
  https://freesound.org/people/ivolipa/sounds/325301/ — 16.06 s from 12 s into
  the preview, the seam crossfaded over 0.25 s so it loops, mono 96 kbit/s.
- `boat-wake-loop.mp3`: “Boat_waves.wav” by Lydmakeren,
  https://freesound.org/people/Lydmakeren/sounds/510900/
