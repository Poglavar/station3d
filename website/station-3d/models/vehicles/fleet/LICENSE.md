# Generic road-fleet models

The generic Station3D road-vehicle JSON geometry exports in this directory
were created by Poglavar Svemira and are licensed under the repository's MIT
license. This covers city cars, hatchbacks, saloons, estates, coupes, SUVs,
taxis, pickups, vans, minibuses, buses and trucks.

They are original low-poly vehicle geometry—not OpenStreetMap data or road
network assets. The creator-authored tram, train and aircraft models are
covered by the adjacent `../LICENSE.md`.
